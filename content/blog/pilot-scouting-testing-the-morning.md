Alva starts here: a scouting phase, not a launch.

Over the next few weekends I'll be on the beach from around 5 to 10 AM with a deliberately small setup — a speaker, a paddleboard, a table and a few chairs. No storefront, no menu, no staff. Just showing up at sunrise, being part of the morning, and paying attention.

The idea being tested is simple: mornings on Rio's beaches are underserved. The sunrise paddle crowd in Copacabana and the early fitness classes in Ipanema are already out there before anyone is set up to serve them. If a small, consistent morning presence can earn a crowd, it might be worth building into a real barraca brand. If not, I'll have spent my winter watching sunrises — an acceptable failure mode.

Doing this in winter is intentional. The beach is quieter, the stakes are low, and the people who show up at 6 AM in July are the real morning crowd, not the summer wave. If it works now, summer is upside.

What I'm watching for during the pilot:

- How heavy the setup and breakdown actually is with limited resources
- Whether people stop, stay, and come back
- What the morning crowd asks for that nobody is offering
- Which locations and hours have real gravity

I'll be documenting the whole thing here — the milestones, the mistakes, and what the beach teaches me. First morning out is soon. More after that.
